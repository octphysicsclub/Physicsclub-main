About STEM October Physics Club

STEM October Physics Club is a student-run club dedicated to gathering those with common interest in physics.

WHO WE ARE

The Official Physics Club of October STEM School

The club members engage in various activities, such as competitions, courses, research, and collaborative problem solving. The club provides the environment needed to learn, collaborate, and foster the members’ passion for physics.

"Cultivate the next generation of global physics innovators."

Our Mission

The club aims to fulfill its primary mission of cultivating a group of individuals with expert knowledge and capabilities in the field of physics, thereby contributing to the progression of scientific understanding. This is accomplished through teaching Calculus-based Physics courses, participating in and winning national and international physics competitions, offering a supportive community for members to thrive and apply their scientific knowledge, and enhancing members’ problem-solving and reasoning abilities. Together, these factors develop the next generation of global physics innovators and researchers.

Our Vision

The vision of the physics club is to create a supportive, passionate community for physics enthusiasts through promoting the members’ academic skills, supporting scientific inquiries, and inspiring our members to pursue their interests. Likewise, we aim to share our passion and message to physics enthusiasts all around the globe.

EDUCATIONAL APPROACH

Philosophy of Physics Education

In order to raise a physically-aware generation of future scientists and physicists, all members are exposed to a unique method for physics education. Throughout the year, members are taught calculus and apply it to physical situations, covering a large portion of calculus-based physics while understanding its application in real-life situations. To provide a competitive environment, all members are updated on all available competition and are encouraged to apply. The club also provides any assistance needed to guarantee the members’ excellence.
