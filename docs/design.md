# Editorial pages design contract

- **Subject and direction:** An image-led student physics journal and publication archive. Use the site's existing charcoal surfaces and cobalt identity; let the real article and book covers carry the visual weight.
- **Color roles:** `#121116` page background, `#1a1a20` raised surface, `#ffffff24` rules, `#528bf2` navigation/focus accent, and `#c7ccd6` secondary reading text. No decorative gradients or glass panels.
- **Type:** Keep the existing Montserrat/Inter site stack. Use Montserrat for concise titles and Inter for long reading text; reserve uppercase tracking for small publication labels.
- **Layout:** Stack magazine stories vertically with a wide image above its title and summary. Present publication covers at the left of compact detail rows; keep full author lists and publisher metadata available in disclosure elements.
- **Signature detail:** The journal uses a custom vector spacetime mesh so the physics motif stays sharp at every screen size. Motion is limited to a restrained cover hover and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- **Navigation:** Keep Magazine and Publications available in both desktop navigation and the mobile drawer on every static page.
