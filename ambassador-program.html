IPhR Ambassador Program

The ambassador program aims to promote physics among students worldwide, testing and rewarding your leadership skills!

The current ambassador cohort is now closed; stay tuned for the next season!

How It Works

Four simple steps to becoming an international student leader

1

Register

Sign up as an ambassador, complete the registration form, and obtain your unique ambassador ID and referral link.

2

Promote

Share your referral link on social media, coordinate with physics teachers, or organize school prep clubs.

3

Refer & Support

Earn credit for every participant who registers using your link and assist them with prep materials.

4

Climb & Win

Top ambassadors earn Wolfram-One subscriptions, AoPS course coupons, and certified leadership honors.

Who is eligible to apply?

Anyone with a passion for physics and a desire to promote academic programs like the International Physics Realm (IPhR) can apply. There are no rigid GPA cutoffs, but demonstrated enthusiasm for science, initiative, and community leadership is highly valued.

What are your responsibilities?

As an ambassador, you represent IPhR within your school, STEM club, or local city. You organize study groups, distribute syllabus resources, assist peers in preparing for Round 1 & Round 2, and collaborate with physics instructors.

What rewards do ambassadors receive?

All ambassadors receive official certificates accredited by STEM High School for Boys – 6th of October. Top ambassadors receive 1-year Wolfram-One licenses and valuable course coupons from Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) to advance their academic goals.

How do I track my progress?

When active cohorts open, ambassadors receive access to an automated dashboard where they can see their referral counts, registered students, leaderboard standings, and certificate status in real time.

Connect with Global Peers

Meet and collaborate with high-achieving physics ambassadors from across the globe, expanding your global network and perspective.

Accredited Recognition

Gain verified extracurricular credentials that strengthen your university applications, CV, and profile as an academic leader.

Exclusive Partner Prizes

Win professional computational licenses from Wolfram Research and problem-solving course access from Art of Problem Solving.
