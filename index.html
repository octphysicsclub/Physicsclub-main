Year-long Academy

General Physics Training Program

Trainees master calculus-based general physics through a comprehensive 40-session curriculum. With independent study, rigorous problem assignments, and 1-on-1 academic mentorship, members build an unstoppable foundation in classical mechanics, thermodynamics, oscillations, and electromagnetism.

Learn More
Computational Physics

COMPASS Program

COMPASS equips students with computational physics skills to numerically solve and simulate complex systems that cannot be solved analytically. The program emphasizes Python programming, numerical differential equations, Monte Carlo methods, and scientific visualizations in classical and quantum mechanics.

Apply Now
Global Competition

IPhR Competition

International Physics Realm (IPhR) is an annual individual physics competition for high school students in grades 9–12 across 70+ nations. Consisting of two fast-paced rounds of challenging physics problems, IPhR tests conceptual depth and rewards finalists with international certificates and medals.

Competition Details
School Bowl

STEM October Physics Bowl

A thrilling one-day club bowl where Grade 10 students partner with experienced club members to tackle fast-paced trivia, physics challenges, and collaborative problem-solving. Experience a day filled with camaraderie, intellectual rivalry, and physics fun.

Experimental Physics

EPORA Program

EPORA gives high school students the opportunity to experience physics beyond the classroom through hands-on experimental laboratory sessions. In partnership with leading Egyptian universities, students engage with real experimental setups, conduct measurements, and analyze results while gaining practical experience with the methods used to investigate physical phenomena.

Are you eager to simulate orbits, wave packets, and chaotic pendulums with Python? Discover how the COMPASS research division prepares students for computational modeling and science careers.
