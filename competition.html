What is IPhR?

IPhR is an annual international physics competition organized by the STEM October Physics Club. It tests your conceptual depth, mathematical rigor, and real-world intuition in calculus-based general physics.

With participants joining from 70+ countries and thousands of schools worldwide, IPhR offers a world-class platform to measure your problem-solving prowess against the brightest young scientific minds.

Check comprehensive syllabus

Eligibility & Categories

IPhR is an individual online competition open to high school students in grades 9 through 12 (or equivalent pre-university levels).

  • • Junior Division: Grades 9–10 focusing on core Newtonian mechanics, energy, and thermodynamics foundations.
  • • Senior Division: Grades 11–12 covering full calculus-based electromagnetism, rotational mechanics, waves, optics, and modern physics.

SAMPLE PROBLEMS

Experience IPhR Questions

IPhR provides a diverse range of problem sets with varying levels of difficulty that cater to students of all levels.

Question 1 • Mechanics

Which of the following is a valid measuring unit of linear momentum?

A) N • s B) J / m C) kg • m / s² D) W • s
Question 2 • Electromagnetism

Imagine having a non-uniform magnetic field source near a coil. Which of the following would induce current in the coil?

A) Rotating the coil B) Moving the coil C) Varying field strength D) All of the above
Question 3 • Rotational Dynamics

What force would you need to pull in a 100 g Yoyo with a 2 meter string in order to make it revolve once per second? Approximate your answer to the nearest decimal. Assume gravity's effect vanished so the Yoyo does not fall.

Question 4 • Thermodynamics

A spring with a spring constant of k = 1000 N/m is compressed by Δx = 0.1 m and connected to an object. The object is released, falling into a 1 L tank of 25 °C water and coming to rest without losing any energy before impact. What is the final temperature of the water?
