Partners Who Propel Us Forward

We are proud to collaborate with a diverse group of academic institutions, industry leaders, and research organizations.

Wolfram Research

Wolfram Research is one of the world's most respected computer, web, and cloud software companies, empowering mathematical discovery and computational intelligence.

Art of Problem Solving (AoPS)

Art of Problem Solving offers math, science, and reasoning courses, focusing on active learning to prepare students for top-tier colleges and competitive science careers.

NonTrivial

NonTrivial is an online fellowship that identifies ambitious teenagers globally, providing the expert guidance and network they need to solve critical global challenges.

Relay

Relay brings real-time collaboration to markdown knowledge bases, enhancing our academic team's productivity and research sharing.
