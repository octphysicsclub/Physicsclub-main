COMMUNITY IMPACT

How Your Donation Might Help Us Be Better

Enhancing Learning Resources

Donations will allow us to enhance our learning resources, providing members with access to advanced physics materials, virtual labs, and expert guest lectures. These resources significantly deepen their understanding and passion for physics, helping them excel in their academic and future professional endeavors.

Expanding Club Activities

Your contributions will help us expand our club activities, offering more interactive and engaging sessions. With additional funding, we can organize workshops, guest speaker events, and hands-on experiments, providing a richer and more comprehensive learning experience for all our members.

Improving Our International Competition

Donations will help us enhance our international competition (IPhR), enabling us to provide better resources, more comprehensive preparation, and higher-quality experiences for participants. Your support will allow us to offer improved materials, expert coaching, and exciting opportunities, ensuring a rewarding and impactful competition for all involved.

Providing Scholarships and Grants

Donations will enable us to provide scholarships and grants to talented members who need financial assistance. This support ensures that all passionate and dedicated students have the opportunity to participate fully in our club’s activities and competitions, regardless of their financial background.

STEM October Physics Club operates as a fiscally sponsored initiative of Hack Club, a 501(c)(3) organization. Donations made through our official portal are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

The Official Physics Club of STEM High School for Boys – 6th of October. Providing collegiate-level physics training, organizing international competitions, and publishing scientific materials for students worldwide.
